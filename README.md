# Super-Kawaii-Bot
A somewhat general-purpose discord bot with its commands phrased differently than the other ones. It's also very kawaii and will send kawaii messages on startup and shutdown!

## Commands
/set_channel [channel], [command]  ; sets the channel for that specific command.
/kidnap ; sends someone who is in another voice channel into yours
/banish ; sends someone who is in another voice channel into a channel you designate or default banish channel (recommended to have low quality.)
/get-a-room ; sends two people into a pre-designated voice channel, useful for couples talking about other stuff in a voice channel with others.
/coin-flip ; do I need to explain? Flips a coin.
